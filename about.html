My Story

Learning by building

I'm a Computer Science Engineering student interested in software development and technology. I enjoy taking an idea, breaking it down into smaller problems, and turning it into a working application.

My current learning journey focuses on Java, JavaScript, web development, databases, and problem-solving. I am particularly interested in understanding how different parts of a software application work together rather than simply building interfaces.

I believe the best way to improve is to build, make mistakes, understand what went wrong, and try again. My portfolio reflects that approach through projects that I build while learning new technologies.
